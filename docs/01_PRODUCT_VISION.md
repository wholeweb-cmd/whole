# Product Vision

## Executive Summary

Fellow is a conversational DeFi operating system built for Robinhood Chain.

Instead of navigating multiple decentralized applications, users interact with Fellow through natural language.

Users simply describe what they want to accomplish.

Fellow understands their intent, prepares the appropriate transaction, explains the outcome, and asks for approval before execution.

The goal is not to replace decentralized finance.

The goal is to remove its complexity.

---

# Vision

To make decentralized finance as simple as having a conversation.

---

# Mission

Empower anyone to interact with Robinhood Chain using natural language without sacrificing transparency, ownership, or security.

---

# Problem

Today's DeFi experience requires users to understand:

- Wallets
- Token approvals
- Liquidity pools
- Concentrated liquidity
- Price ranges
- Slippage
- Routing
- Gas fees

Even experienced users spend more time learning interfaces than accomplishing their goals.

The complexity comes from software, not finance.

---

# Solution

Fellow introduces an intent-based interaction model.

Instead of learning interfaces, users express goals.

Example:

Swap my ETH into USDG.

Add liquidity to the most active ETH pool.

Claim every fee I have earned.

Move my position closer to market price.

Show my portfolio performance this week.

Fellow converts those intentions into safe, reviewable blockchain transactions.
