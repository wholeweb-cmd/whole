# Fellow Manifesto

## Why Fellow Exists

Decentralized finance has unlocked powerful financial tools, but interacting with them still feels unnecessarily complex.

Users are expected to understand protocols, liquidity pools, token approvals, price ranges, transaction routing, and countless interface patterns before they can perform even the simplest action.

This complexity creates friction.

Fellow exists to remove that friction.

Instead of learning how DeFi works, users simply describe what they want.

Fellow understands the intent, prepares the appropriate on-chain transaction, and lets users remain in complete control through their wallet.

The protocol stays powerful.

The experience becomes effortless.

---

## Our Belief

People should think about outcomes.

Not interfaces.

Not protocols.

Not transaction builders.

Natural language should become the primary interface for decentralized finance.

---

## What Fellow Is

Fellow is a conversational operating system for Robinhood Chain.

Users interact with DeFi by expressing intent in plain language.

Examples:

- Swap my ETH to USDG
- Add liquidity to the best ETH pool
- Claim all my fees
- Move my position closer to market price
- Show my highest earning LP

Every request becomes an executable transaction.

---

## What Fellow Is Not

Fellow is not a wallet.

Fellow is not a DEX.

Fellow is not a custody platform.

Fellow never owns user funds.

Every transaction must always be reviewed and approved by the user.

---

## Core Philosophy

Intent First

Users think about goals.

Fellow handles execution.

---

Conversation Before Interface

Every action should begin naturally.

The interface exists to support the conversation, not replace it.

---

Transparency Always

Users should always understand what will happen before signing a transaction.

Nothing should execute silently.

---

User Sovereignty

Wallet ownership remains with the user.

Fellow never signs transactions.

Never stores private keys.

Never takes custody of assets.

---

Simple Outside

Powerful Inside

The complexity belongs to the software.

Never to the user.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Our goal is not to build another DeFi application.

Our goal is to become the default interaction layer between humans and decentralized finance.

When people think about using DeFi,

they shouldn't think about protocols.

They should think about talking to Fellow.
